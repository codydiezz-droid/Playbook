    Training

    Your week, day by day

    10K build · 9 → 21 km a week, then taper

    Distances in weeks 1–3 assume an easy pace of about 6:45–7:15/km. Stop at whichever comes first, time or distance. After the week 3 test: 3 km pace of 5:15/km or faster means you're on track; slower than 5:30 means threshold and race-pace work move to 5:45–5:50/km.

    Progression

    1. Start each lift at the bottom of the rep range at the target RIR (reps left in the tank).
    2. Add reps each week until every set hits the top of the range.
    3. Then add weight: +2.5–5 lb upper body, +5–10 lb deadlift/squat if the bar still moved fast.
    4. Same weight and reps three sessions in a row? Drop 10% and build back up.

    Let WHOOP set the effort

    Green · 67%+

    Do the plan as written.

    Yellow · 34–66%

    Do it, but stop at 2 reps in reserve or RPE 7. Under 50%: the quality run becomes an easy run.

    Red · under 34%

    Swap the session for a 30-min walk and mobility. Same wake time, lights out no later than 12:45.

    Missed a workout?

    • Never miss twice. The next session happens, even as the minimum version.
    • No doubling up to catch up.
    • Missed legs: do it Wednesday before an easy run, or skip it. Never within 72h of Friday soccer.
    • Missed push or pull: next free day (Wed or Sun), or skip.
    • Missed Wednesday run: Thursday, easy only. Missed Sunday long run: skip it, never Monday before soccer.
